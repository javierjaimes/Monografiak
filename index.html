Monografiak is an independent press. Each monograph is one long-form essay with figures, equations and references. It is signed by its author and published with a fingerprint of its source text. Each one expires a year after publication, on purpose.

Here the text is the design: three typefaces, three colours and thin rules. Mathematics is native MathML, so it can be read aloud and needs no scripts. Every hash printed on these pages is real and can be reproduced.
